e-citizenship – Sabourg https://www.sabourg.com Fri, 19 Dec 2025 17:46:34 +0000 en-GB hourly 1 https://wordpress.org/?v=6.9 Official presentation of the Digital Foundation ../../../../en/2025/10/08/official-presentation-of-the-digital-foundation-2/ Wed, 08 Oct 2025 16:07:53 +0000 ../../../../?p=1471 On May 5, during Orthodox Easter celebrations, the Prince-Abbot of Sabourg, His Most Reverend Highness Bishop Jean Luc, presented the digitization of the State of Sabourg through the creation of the Sabourg Digital Foundation.To this end, 144,000 digital identities called e-sbr, or tokens made available to all citizens of Sabourg and future digital residents of Sabourg, have been issued. The acquisition of these tokens will be activated by December 13, 2024, Sabourg’s national holiday, with the presentation of the program that will give access to the formal application for all those who wish to participate in the digitization of the abbatial state by donating a sum of money that will be used for economic development to carry out the major projects of the Principality of Sabourg.
