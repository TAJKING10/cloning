The sixth anniversary of our coronation marks a more intense commitment for all members of the Council of Departments, our Minister Councillors. Taking the oath in Square San Martino, in the presence of the Patron Saint of Seborga, as our Secretary of State has donated a relic that preserves part of his holy body, makes your commitment and that of the highest dignitaries of the Principality of Sabourg sacred.

The year 2026 will define the affirmation of the monastic state for the general public, as the states of the international community, fascinated by your work, will accept the long-awaited official recognition.

Entrust your souls to the Holy Spirit, so that the eternal light may strengthen your spirits for this divine mission of charity in the service of the Most High.

Dedicate a few minutes of your day to reciting Psalm 91, which is our motto: Sub Unbra Sedi.

Fraternal blessings

Giovanni Luca Pietro II

]]> Feast day of St. Michael the Archangel, patron saint of Sabourg ../../../../en/2025/10/09/feast-day-of-st-michael-the-archangel-patron-saint-of-sabourg/ Thu, 09 Oct 2025 13:20:00 +0000 ../../../../?p=1504 On September 28, 2025, the feast day of St. Michael the Archangel, patron saint of Sabourg, was celebrated.
The event began in the morning at the Port of Ventimiglia with the presentation of the e-residence card by the Minister of Communication, followed by a concert by the Abbaziale Gendarmerie Orchestra. Next, the director of the Maritime Division presented the Yacht Club project, which will be officially inaugurated in the summer of 2026.
After the official presentations, His Excellency the Prince-Abbot of Sabourg appointed His Excellency Monsignor Marc Vincent Vattier as Vice President of the Constitutional Court, ending his term as Prime Minister, and Professor Vicenzo Zagari took over as President of the Supreme Court. Senior Councilor Prof. Mario Porrone, former Minister of Education, was appointed interim Prime Minister. H.E. Mons. Andrea de Lucia was appointed Vice President of the Crown Council and handed over the ancient regulations of the Priory of St. Michael to H.E. Rev. Mons Svetoslav Vasilev, assuming the position of Secretary of State Prior of St. Michael. After the institutional appointments, the Prince of Sabourg conferred the honors of the Order of St. Martin and the Valour of the Abbey Gendarmerie.
In the afternoon, at the Chapel of St. Michael in VIcolo degli Staggi in Ventimiglia, the consecrations of the Monastic Order took place: Order of St. Benedict Minor, Order of St. Mary of Magdala. The Grand Commander of the Equestrian Order of St. Martin of the Grand Commendation of the Democratic Republic of Congo and Lieutenant of Kinshasa was also consecrated.

The ceremony concluded with the celebration of the Divine Liturgy of St. John Chrysostom.
