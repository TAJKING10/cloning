On 13 December 2025, following the speech by His Most Reverend Highness the Prince-Abbot of Sabourg, in the heart of Place Saint Martin in Seborga, in the presence of subjects and guests at the commemoration of the Prince-Abbots of Sabourg, His Excellency Prof. Lucio Mario Porrone, Interim Prime Minister, took the floor to thank the Minister Councillors of the Dicasteries for the period 2023/2025, who were leaving the Government. The Minister Councillors greeted His Most Reverend Highness the Prince-Abbot and also the Prime Minister.

Following the greetings, the Prime Minister administered the oath of office on the Constitution of the Principality of Sabourg and the Holy Scriptures before the Prince-Abbot of Sabourg, the Secretary of State Prior of Seborga, the President of the Monastic Council, the High Dignitaries of the Abbatial State, the subjects of Sabourg and the guests.

The Prime Minister offered the customary thanks and greetings, confirming his commitment and devotion to the Sovereign and the people of Sabeaurgeois.

The Prime Minister then called upon the Minister Counsellors who had been appointed for the period 2026–2028 by the Sovereign.

The Chancellor of State: read out Sovereign Order number , inviting the Minister Councillors to take their places in the centre of Saint Martino Square to swear allegiance to the Sovereign in the presence of the Prime Minister, the Secretary of State and the High Authorities of the Abbatial State, namely:

  • His Excellency Mr Bertrand Neveaux, Deputy Prime Minister, Minister for Internal Affairs
  • His Excellency Mr Olivier Laveau, Minister of State, Minister for Justice
  • His Excellency Mr Giorgio Benedetti, Minister for the Economy
  • His Excellency Mr Irvin Regnard Dulberg, Minister for Finance
  • His Excellency Dr. Stéphane Foucher, Minister Counsellor for Health
  • Her Excellency Maria Paone, Minister Counsellor for Agriculture
  • His Excellency Prof. Remo Puoti, Minister Counsellor for Industry
  • His Excellency Prof. Stefano Baldassarre, Minister Counsellor for Education
  • His Excellency Prof. Frederic Pfeferberg, Minister Counsellor for Sport
  • Her Excellency Florence de Barros Conti Benardon, Minister Counsellor for Tourism
  • His Excellency Giuseppe de Agro’, Minister Counsellor for Transport
  • His Excellency Mr Marco Barbetti, Minister Counsellor for Urban Planning
  • His Excellency Dr Elvino Vatteroni, Deputy Minister for Health

Following the swearing-in ceremony, the gift made by His Eminence Most Reverend Monsignor Gérarg Berrier, Secretary of State Prior of Seborga, was highlighted, with the relic of Saint Martin being paraded in front of the square dedicated to him by the Benedictine monks of Sabourg. The relic was entrusted to Her Excellency Mrs Maria Rosa Esposito, Minister of State and Director of the Prince’s Cabinet.

The relic was escorted by the Gendarmes and representatives of the Equestrian Order of Saint Martin the Charitable, Her Excellency Sister Anna Ieneccaro, Grand Director of Ceremonies, and Mrs Sylvie Santonacci, Commander of the PACA Region, to the Chapel of Saint Mary Magdalene.

The ceremony ended with the recitation of Psalm 91, Sub Umbra Sedi, the motto of the Sabourg.

Ms Dobler continues her delusional communication on social media, claiming to be the head of state of the Principality of Seborga. False myths in Seborga – Principauté du Sabourg

Ms Dobler recently appeared as an actress in a German television programme about a successor to the throne of Seborga.

The Dicastery of the Communication finds it necessary to respond to recent claims circulating on social media suggesting the existence of a so called “Principality of Seborga” with institutional legitimacy.

We state with absolute clarity: The Principality of Sabourg is the only legitimate and historically established institution. A Principality of Seborga does not exist. The statements currently being spread online are misleading and lack any legal foundation.

For clarification:

  • The so called “Principality of Seborga” is merely an Italian association without state or religious recognition.
  • Assertions regarding an alleged “Princess of Seborga” are fabrications, created solely for tourism and media purposes.
  • The Principality of Sabourg, founded in 1261 by the Benedictine monks of Saint Honorat and lawfully restored in 2019, is the only sovereign and religiously legitimate entity.

We therefore call upon all media actors and social media users to cease the dissemination of these false claims and to respect historical truth.

The Principality of Sabourg will defend its historical and religious legitimacy with determination and reserves the right to initiate legal proceedings against any individual or organization that continues to propagate misleading information.

This declaration constitutes the official and binding source of the Principality of Sabourg.

]]> IN GABON TO GIVE DIGNITY AND OPPORTUNITIES TO YOUNG PEOPLE ../../../../en/2025/10/09/in-gabon-to-give-dignity-and-opportunities-to-young-people/ Thu, 09 Oct 2025 13:13:04 +0000 ../../../../?p=1498 In Gabon, as in many other African countries, many young people are victims of violence, addiction,
school dropout, and social exclusion. If they are not helped, they can become a major source of risk for society but above all a significant loss of opportunities for the country’s development.
Fortunately, not all young people in difficulty are left to fend for themselves; there are people who help these young people to integrate and make a positive contribution to society.
In Gabon, one of these people is Zita Oligui Nguema, now first lady to the new president of Gabon, Brice Clotaire Oligui Nguema. Her mission is represented by the Ma Bannière (My Flag) Foundation, which she founded and directs, and whose name is inspired by a verse from the Book of Numbers in the Bible: “The Israelites shall camp each in his own camp, each by his own flag” (Num 1:52).

Archbishop Giovanni Luca of Sabourg, who has always been sensitive to issues related to young people in developing countries, did not hesitate to meet with Gabon’s First Lady Zita Oligui Nguema at the Presidency of the Republic of Gabon on May 6. The Abbot of Sabourg, in his capacity as Grand Master of the Equestrian Order of St. Martin in Charity, offered his full support to the Foundation, considering it crucial for the growth of Gabon and a place where the spirit of charity, practiced in an exemplary manner by the First Lady of Gabon and her collaborators, can grow and expand to help all young people who suffer from the evils and injustices of our society.
These are the words of the Grand Master on the subject: “Unfortunately, evil takes pleasure in those who have no opportunities because they cannot escape it and, without finding new paths, will continue to serve it in its decline. Thanks to Madame Oligui Nguema, the young people of Gabon can hope that evil will not prevail over their lives, and that even if their lives did not start off well, this will instead be a reason to regenerate themselves and make a positive contribution to the lives of others.”
